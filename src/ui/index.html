Reading these numbers

A short queue can occur when many tabs open together. Rejected and failed requests are totals for the session; an old failure does not mean the connection is failing now. Look for counts that keep rising alongside loading problems.

USB response time measures the local phone relay. The HTTPS check tests a small request through the phone. Neither proves every app is using this connection.

Before you connect

Three steps to get your iPhone ready.

  1. Connect your iPhone with a USB-C cable and unlock it.
  2. Turn cellular on. Turn Wi-Fi and Personal Hotspot off.
  3. Keep iSH open with PHONE NETWORK READY showing.
python3 /root/phone_network.py
Setup & maintenancePair your iPhone or update the phone relay.

1. Prepare and pair

Checking pairing…
  1. Install Apple Devices on Windows and iSH Shell on your iPhone.
  2. In iSH, enter apk add python3 and wait for localhost:~#.
  3. Connect one iPhone by USB. Unlock it and tap Trust if asked, then pair it below.

A private pairing key stays encrypted for this Windows account.

2. Prepare the receiver

Disconnect in this app yourself. In iSH, press Ctrl + C if the relay is running. Prepare the command, then paste it into iSH.

Send the relay

Once iSH says READY FOR PHONE UPDATE, send the update. The receiver waits three minutes; rerun the command if it expires.

3. Start the relay

When iSH says Saved, run the command below. Use Check phone above when the relay is ready.

python3 /root/phone_network.py

The previous script is saved as phone_network.py.backup. Sending an update does not connect this PC.

AboutHow it works, privacy and licensing.1.2.0

An iSH relay and a Windows client for carrying PC traffic through your iPhone over USB. Keep iSH visible and the phone unlocked.

Educational Purposes Only

For noncommercial education, personal study and authorized testing. Follow your carrier agreement and applicable laws. This notice does not provide a legal exemption or permission to bypass plan restrictions.

Carrier billing and hotspot classification have not been verified. The project does not promise unlimited data, undetectability, or carrier approval.

Connection behavior

Manual connection
Check phone tests USB and HTTPS. Connect PC changes routing after Windows administrator approval. Opening the app never connects.
Internet fallback
Other enabled connections can still be used. This is not a VPN or kill switch, and it does not guarantee every app uses the phone.
Carrier usage
Carrier billing and hotspot classification have not been verified. Unlimited usage is not guaranteed.
Compatibility
Requires Apple device support, WebView2, iSH with Python, and a paired iPhone. Some games, VPNs and interface-bound apps may not work.

Privacy & pairing

The paired device is pinned. Pairing is encrypted for this Windows account; the PC gateway accepts local connections only and uses a per-session password. HTTPS validation stays enabled.

No browsing-history log or telemetry. Local session files hold counters and diagnostic errors. Do not publish pairing files, receiver commands, session folders or old test data.
